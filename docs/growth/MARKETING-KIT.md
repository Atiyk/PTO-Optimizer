## Rules for every piece

- Name: Timebower. Never Gapless or Days Off.
- Every total comes with its cost: "27 days off for 7 PTO days", never "27 days off" alone. "Days off" includes weekends and holidays; say so the first time in each piece.
- Every holiday example states the assumption: "if your employer observes it". Juneteenth, Columbus Day, Veterans Day and the Friday-observed Christmas and New Year's are the ones people most often don't get.
- Numbers come from the live planner (cite as "Timebower output") or from a hand count you have checked against a calendar. The examples below were checked with `node` Date and against OPM's 2027 federal holiday list.
- No testimonials, user counts, ratings, "as seen in", or invented quotes.
- Search titles and social captions may say "vacation days" because people search for it. The product UI says "PTO days".

## Community participation guidelines

1. **Read each community's rules before posting.** Check the sidebar and pinned rules of every subreddit, forum or group, every time. Many finance and career subreddits ban self-promotion or links entirely. If the rules say no, don't post, or answer without a link.
2. **Disclose, every time.** "I made this" or "I built Timebower" in any post or comment that mentions it.
3. **Value first.** The post must be useful if the link is removed: give the dates and the assumptions in the text itself.
4. **One account, the owner's own.** No sock puppets, no asking friends to upvote or comment, no posting the same text in many communities on one day.
5. **Answer existing questions more than you start threads.** A good answer to "how should I use my last 3 PTO days" beats a launch post.
6. **Hacker News:** Show HN is for things people can try now; Timebower needs no signup, which fits. Post once, from the owner's account, and stay to answer comments. Never ask for upvotes.
7. **Product Hunt:** Follow PH's launch guidelines; don't ask for upvotes, don't use upvote groups. Telling people the launch is happening is fine.
8. **Corrections in public.** If someone finds a wrong date or number, thank them, fix or file it, and edit the post.
9. **No HR or legal advice.** Say "check your employer's policy" rather than telling people what they're entitled to.
10. **Never post anyone's real request, manager's name or company calendar** in a demo.

## Product description

**Short (about 140 characters):** Timebower shows which days to take off so your PTO joins weekends and holidays, then writes the request. Free, no account.

**Long:**

Timebower is a free planner for paid time off. Tell it how many PTO days you have and which holidays your company gives you, and it shows the breaks where a few PTO days join weekends and holidays into the most days off. Each break lists the exact days to request, the date to ask by, and a message you can send your manager. Mark it requested, then approved, and Timebower keeps your balance and expiry dates in view. It works for 204 countries, plans around school breaks and a partner's time off, and exports to your calendar. The same inputs always give the same plan, and your settings stay in your browser.

## Marketing FAQ copy

For landing pages, posts and press replies. The in-app FAQ (`src/ui/FAQ.tsx`) stays as it is.

**Is it really free?** Yes. There's no account and no paid tier today.

**What does "days off" mean?** Every calendar day you're not working in a break: weekends, holidays and PTO days together. "27 days off for 7 PTO days" means you request 7 days.

**My company doesn't give every federal holiday. Does it still work?** Yes. Uncheck the ones you work and add your company's own days, like the day after Thanksgiving. The plan changes to match.

**Does it send the request for me?** No. It writes the message and the list of dates; you send it through whatever your company uses. Then you can mark it requested and approved.

**Where does my data go?** Employer holidays, request notes and saved plans stay in your browser. A plan link carries your planning settings, so anyone you send it to sees that plan.

**Why do other sites say 10 PTO days turns into 55 days off?** Totals depend on what you count. Timebower counts only the days inside each break, and shows the PTO cost next to every total.
