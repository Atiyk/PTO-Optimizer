# New York child day care centers: supervision (excerpt)

(k) The following minimum staff to child ratios and maximum group sizes must be maintained:

| Age of children | Staff to child ratio | Maximum group size |
|---|---|---|
| 6 weeks to 18 months | 1:4 | 8 |
| 18 months to 36 months | 1:5 | 12 |
| 3 years | 1:7 | 18 |
| 4 years | 1:8 | 21 |
| 5 years | 1:9 | 24 |

No person under 18 years of age may be left alone in charge of a group of children.
