# Washington: paid sick leave and overtime (excerpt)

RCW 49.46.210(1)(a) Beginning January 1, 2018, every employer shall provide each of its employees paid sick leave at a minimum rate of accrual of at least one hour of paid sick leave for every forty hours worked as an employee.
RCW 49.46.210(1)(d) An employee is entitled to use accrued paid sick leave beginning on the ninetieth calendar day after the commencement of his or her employment.
RCW 49.46.130(1) No employer shall employ any of his or her employees for a workweek longer than forty hours unless such employee receives compensation for employment in excess of the hours above specified at a rate not less than one and one-half times the regular rate at which he or she is employed.
