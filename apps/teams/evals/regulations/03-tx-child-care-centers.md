# Texas licensed child-care centers: ratio and group size (excerpt)

For a group of children all the same age, you must follow these ratios and maximum group sizes:

| Age of children | Child/caregiver ratio | Maximum group size |
|---|---|---|
| 0 through 11 months | 1:4 | 10 |
| 12 through 17 months | 1:5 | 13 |
| 18 through 23 months | 1:9 | 18 |
| 2 years | 1:11 | 22 |
| 3 years | 1:15 | 30 |
| 4 years | 1:18 | 35 |
