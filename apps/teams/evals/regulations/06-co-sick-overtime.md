# Colorado: Healthy Families and Workplaces Act and COMPS overtime (excerpt)

8-13.3-403(2)(a) An employee accrues at least one hour of paid sick leave for every thirty hours worked, up to a maximum of forty-eight hours of paid sick leave in a year.
8-13.3-403(3)(b) An employer is not required to allow an employee to use more than forty-eight hours of paid sick leave in a year.
Rule 4.1.1 Employees shall be paid time and one-half of the regular rate of pay for any work in excess of 40 hours per workweek or 12 hours per workday.
