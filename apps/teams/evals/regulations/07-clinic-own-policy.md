# Example Dental Group: clinic staffing policy

1. Hygiene: one hygienist for every 8 hygiene appointments in a half day.
2. At least one staff member with a current BLS certification must be present on site whenever patients are seen.
3. Staff must have at least 10 hours of rest between shifts.
4. No employee may be scheduled for more than 10 hours in a workday.
5. No employee may work more than 6 consecutive days.
6. Rotas are published two weeks ahead.
