Brightside Ltd — Staff Handbook

Section 9: Holidays

9.1 Your holiday entitlement is 25 days per holiday year, plus bank holidays.
9.2 The holiday year runs from 1 April to 31 March.
9.3 You may carry forward a maximum of 5 days into the next holiday year with your manager's agreement; any days carried forward must be taken by the 30th of June.
9.4 Holiday requests should be made at least two weeks in advance.

Section 10: Sickness absence
10.1 Please call your line manager before 10am on the first day of absence.
