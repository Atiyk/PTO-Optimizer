SUNRISE CAFE — TEAM MEMBER GUIDE

Paid Time Off (PTO)
All team members earn 1 hour of PTO for every 30 hours worked. PTO accrues each pay period; we pay biweekly.
Accrual stops once your PTO balance reaches a cap of 80 hours, and starts again after you use some.
New team members can use accrued PTO after completing 90 days of employment.
Unused PTO rolls over to the next year, subject to the cap.

Scheduling
Shift swaps must be approved by the shift lead.
