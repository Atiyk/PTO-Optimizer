Privacy

Your PTO records stay in this browser. Shared links and files contain the information you choose to share.

What this site does with your data

PTO planning and balance calculations run in your browser. Saved employer calendars, policy settings, and personal request records are stored in this browser without an account. They are not automatically synchronized to another device. You can remove saved records using the relevant reset or delete controls, or by clearing this site's browser data.

Public holiday data is served from this site's own static files first. The only time a request leaves your browser for it is as a fallback, when a country or year isn't available from those static files — that request goes to Nager.Date and contains no personal information, just a country code and a year.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Plausible for aggregate page views and named feature actions, such as using a forecast or an export. We remove query parameters and URL fragments before sending analytics events. These events include the public page route, not your PTO balance, leave dates, request notes, approval status, or policy inputs. We skip analytics when an incoming referrer URL contains query parameters or a fragment.

Analytics providers may process browser and device information and approximate location to produce aggregate statistics. Our pages prevent the full planning URL from being sent in outgoing referrer headers.

Links you share

A planning or policy link can include the dates, PTO budget, and settings encoded in its URL. Anyone with the link can read the included settings; opening a link also sends that URL to the hosting service as a normal page request. Personal request notes and tracker statuses are kept out of planning links. Calendar files and images are created on your device. Review the dates and balance visibility before sharing a link or image.
