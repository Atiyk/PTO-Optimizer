Your time off is personal. Your planning should be, too.

This page explains what stays in your browser, what a shared link reveals, and how basic site analytics and holiday lookups work.

Most planning runs on your device.

No account is required. Calendar subscriptions and optional email reminders send your plan link to Gapless’s server, which builds the calendar or email from it. If you sign in for reminders, Gapless stores your email address, the plan or policy links you save, and when it last emailed you. Personal PTO records are not uploaded or synced between devices.

What stays in your browser

Balance settings, employer calendars, and tracked PTO requests are saved locally in your browser when you use the relevant features. The planner and its calculations work without a user account. These records are not automatically synchronized across browsers or devices.

Downloaded calendar files and share images are generated on your device. We do not automatically upload them. A calendar subscription is different: your calendar app (Google, Apple or Microsoft) keeps the subscription link and asks Gapless’s server for the latest calendar each time it refreshes.

Calendar subscriptions

When you subscribe to a plan or expiry calendar, your calendar app (and its provider, such as Google, Apple or Microsoft) keeps the subscription link and fetches it regularly. Gapless’s server rebuilds the calendar from the settings in that link each time; it does not store the link. Anyone with the link can see those settings.

What happens when you share a plan

A planning or policy URL may encode dates, PTO balances, employer holidays, and other chosen settings. Anyone with that link can read those settings. Opening the URL also sends it to the hosting provider as part of a normal web request. We do not put personal request notes or tracker approval statuses in shared planning URLs.

Before sharing: check the dates, URL and any balance-visibility controls. You can choose not to share any plan or export.

If you turn on email reminders

Signing in is optional and only adds reminder emails and forwarding. Gapless emails you a one-time sign-in link; asking for a link does not create an account. Once you sign in, it stores your email address, the plan or policy link you choose to save, whether reminders are on, and the date of the last reminder. Your notice period, balance and planned days are inside those links, exactly as in a link you share.

Emails are sent through Gmail. If you forward an approval email to Gapless, it passes through CloudMailin, is read once to write a reply to your own address, and is not stored. Every reminder has a link to stop reminders, and “Delete my account” removes everything at once. Reminder emails are best-effort and can be skipped on days the site reaches its email limit.

Lightweight usage analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Plausible to understand aggregate page visits and feature interactions. The analytics integration strips query strings and URL fragments from reported page URLs and does not intentionally send PTO balances, request details or policy values as event properties.

Analytics providers may receive browser and device information and approximate location as part of operating their services. Gapless suppresses analytics for incoming referrer URLs containing query parameters or fragments, and uses a no-referrer policy for outgoing links.

Where holiday information comes from

Gapless first uses holiday data served with the site. When a requested country and year are not available locally, the app may request public holiday information from Nager.Date. That lookup uses the country and year, not your PTO balance, tracked requests, or notes.

Your choices and controls

  • Use Gapless without signing in. Sign in only if you want reminder emails, and delete your account in one click.
  • Delete saved PTO requests, policy settings, or employer-calendar preferences with the controls provided where you entered them.
  • Clear this site's browser storage if you want to remove local settings and records from that browser.
  • Avoid including private details in any URL, exported file or email you send to someone else.

Clearing browser storage does not retract a plan link or file you previously shared with someone else. See our Terms of use for limits on the planner’s estimates.

Questions or feedback?
