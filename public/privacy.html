Your time off is personal. Your planning should be, too.

This page explains what stays in your browser, what a shared link reveals, and how basic site analytics and holiday lookups work.

Most planning runs on your device.

No account is required. Calendar subscriptions send your chosen dates (not your balance) to Timebower’s server, which builds the calendar from them. Optional email reminders send your plan link. If you sign in for reminders, Timebower stores your email address, the plan or policy links you save, and when it last emailed you. Personal PTO records are not uploaded or synced between devices.

What stays in your browser

Private planner and policy drafts stay in the current browser tab. If you save a policy or employer calendar or track a PTO request, the record is stored in your browser. The planner works without an account. These records are not automatically synchronized across devices.

Records kept in this browser’s storage include: saved policy settings, saved employer calendars, tracked PTO requests, saved plans you pin for comparison, and the shared My PTO setting for whether your entered balance already includes approved leave. When you follow a “Plan the days” link from My PTO, private dates such as manually planned days are handed to the planner through this tab’s session storage, not the link, and are removed once the planner opens.

Downloaded calendar files and share images are generated on your device. We do not automatically upload them. A calendar subscription is different: your calendar app (Google, Apple or Microsoft) keeps the subscription link and asks Timebower’s server for the calendar each time it refreshes.

Calendar subscriptions

Your calendar app and its provider keep the subscription link and fetch it regularly, and the hosting provider may log it. A subscription is a snapshot: it shows the dates as they were when you subscribed and does not change when you edit your plan or policy. Subscribe again after changes.

What a plan subscription link contains: your chosen break dates and which of those days are PTO, your country and region, the plan’s start and end dates, your work week and alternate-Friday start date if you use one, the date you made the link, and observed holiday dates only if you choose to include them. It does not contain your PTO balance or budget, accrual, expiry, carryover or notice settings, or employer holiday names.

What an expiry reminder subscription link contains: the reminder dates, the last day to use the PTO they warn about, the suggested days to use it, and the date you made the link. It does not contain your balance or policy settings, and the calendar does not say how many days are at risk.

Subscription links made before November 2026 carried the plan or policy settings instead and still work. To replace one, subscribe again and remove the old calendar. Anyone with a subscription link can read what it contains.

What happens when you share a plan

A new public planner link includes the scenario dates, PTO budget and public planning controls. Employer names, private calendar settings, tracked requests and personal policy details stay in your browser by default. If you choose to share observed holiday dates, the link includes those dates without employer labels. Legacy links and policy links you explicitly create for reminders may carry additional details. Opening any link sends its contents to the hosting provider as part of the normal web request.

Before sharing: check the dates, URL and any balance-visibility controls. You can choose not to share any plan or export.

If you turn on email reminders

Signing in is optional and only adds reminder emails and forwarding. Timebower emails you a one-time sign-in link; asking for a link does not create an account. Once you sign in, it stores your email address, the plan or policy link you choose to save, whether reminders are on, and the date of the last reminder. A plan reminder can use a public scenario snapshot. A policy reminder can contain your balance and policy assumptions when you explicitly choose to send those settings.

Emails are sent through Gmail. If you forward an approval email to Timebower, it passes through CloudMailin, is read once to write a reply to your own address, and is not stored. Every reminder has a link to stop reminders, and “Delete my account” removes everything at once. Reminder emails are best-effort and can be skipped on days the site reaches its email limit.

Lightweight usage analytics

Timebower uses Vercel Web Analytics and Plausible to count page visits and which features get used, in total. Neither uses cookies, and Timebower gives you no analytics ID. Reported page addresses keep only the public page path, such as /pto, with the rest of the address removed, so your balance, dates, holidays and settings in a plan link are never sent. Events are bare names with no properties attached.

One exception, for Plausible page visits only: if you arrive through a campaign link, the labels utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign are kept when each is a short plain word (for example utm_source=newsletter). Anything else, including email addresses, dates and numbers, is dropped. This tells us which posts or newsletters bring people to Timebower. Vercel never receives these labels.

These are every event name Timebower can send:

  • Planning: quick_plan_engaged, landing_plan_opened, guide_plan_opened, meaningful_plan_interaction, Solve completed, holiday_check_opened, holiday_preference_customized, team_days_used, plan_saved.
  • First time you take a kind of break action, remembered in your browser: first_break_saved, first_break_tracked, first_break_exported, first_break_shared, second_meaningful_break_action.
  • Requests and exports: request_text_copied, request_tracking_started, request_status_updated, ICS downloaded, feed_subscribed, Image copied, portrait_image_exported.
  • Sharing: URL shared, shared_link_opened, shared_plan_made_own, Overlap link opened, partner_share_generated, partner_share_opened, partner_proposal_generated.
  • School and family coverage: family_source_applied, family_coverage_priority_changed, family_share_generated, family_share_opened, family_listed_coverage_confirmed.
  • Policy and glossary: balance_forecast_used, expiration_alert_viewed, expiration_alert_action_taken, policy_tool_used, policy_alert_action_clicked, term_opened, paragraph_decoded, policy_questions_copied.
  • Coming back: returned_with_saved_url, at most once per browser tab session, when you reopen a plan link you kept or visit with a saved plan.

Analytics providers may receive browser and device information and approximate location as part of running their services. Timebower sends no analytics at all when you arrive from a link whose address has query parameters or a fragment, and uses a no-referrer policy for links you follow out of Timebower.

Where holiday information comes from

Timebower first uses holiday data served with the site. When a requested country and year are not available locally, the app may request public holiday information from Nager.Date. That lookup uses the country and year, not your PTO balance, tracked requests, or notes.

Your choices and controls

  • Use Timebower without signing in. Sign in only if you want reminder emails, and delete your account in one click.
  • Delete saved PTO requests, policy settings, or employer-calendar preferences with the controls provided where you entered them.
  • Clear this site's browser storage if you want to remove local settings and records from that browser.
  • Avoid including private details in any URL, exported file or email you send to someone else.

Clearing browser storage does not retract a plan link or file you previously shared with someone else. See our Terms of use for limits on the planner’s estimates.

Questions or feedback?
