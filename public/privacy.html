Privacy

Short version: nothing about you is collected.

What this tool does with your data

PTO Optimizer runs entirely in your browser. The dates, PTO balance, country, and every other setting you enter stay on your device and are never sent to a server — there isn't one. The only thing that leaves your browser is a request to Nager.Date to look up public holidays for the country you select, which contains no personal information, just a country code and a year.

Analytics

We use Plausible, a cookieless analytics service. It sets no cookies and stores no cross-site identifier tied to you — visit counts are derived from a rotating, salted daily hash that's discarded after 24 hours. We track six anonymous events (page views and which export/share actions were used) and nothing else: no inputs, no dates, no locations, no plan details.

Links you share

If you copy a share link, download a calendar file, or copy an image, that file or link is created on your own device and only goes wherever you choose to send it — we never see it.
